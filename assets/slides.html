The attack is a script

  • Authority pretext: “I am Inspector Sharma from the CBI…”
  • False accusation: Aadhaar linked to a narcotics case
  • Isolation: “Do not tell anyone, stay on this video call”
  • Fund verification: transfer to an “RBI account”
  • Extraction: irreversible UPI / RTGS transfers

Why existing tools fail

  • Caller ID and blocklists decide before the call — the fraud happens inside it.
  • US/EU elder-fraud products screen or terminate calls, US-first, no Indian scripts.
  • Government portals act after the money is gone.
  • Voice cloning now needs only 3–5 seconds of public audio.

Nobody acts while the victim is still on the line.

What Rakshak does — in the middle of the call

1 · Listen

Deepgram Nova-3 transcribes the call in real time. A Featherless open-weight LLM classifies family + stage against a genome of known scripts.

2 · Intervene

At the isolation or payment cue: guardian voice (Kokoro, Hindi/English), a stop card, and a live alert to the family war room.

3 · Fight back

The counter-agent takes over as a slow, hard-of-hearing persona, stalls the scammer and makes them repeat payment details.

4 · Recover

Golden-hour pack: timeline, red flags, caller identifiers, pre-filled complaint draft for cybercrime.gov.in / 1930, bank + UPI freeze checklist.

5 · Learn

Every confirmed call contributes to the Scam Genome — script fingerprints and scammer identifiers. Blocklists can’t compound; this can.

How it works

Parent Shield (PWA) │ audio + events over WebSocket ▼ Durable Object “CallAgent” ── Deepgram Nova-3 STT │ Featherless DeepSeek-V4-Flash │ Kokoro TTS (guardian + decoy) │ script state machine + policy │ identifier extraction (words→digits, fuzzy UPI) ▼ Family War Room ◄── live transcript, alerts, actions Golden-hour pack ◄── evidence bundle + complaint draft Scam Genome ◄── GenomeAgent DO (scripts + identifier index)

Sponsor tech at the centre

Featherless runs the fraud classifier and both synthetic voices through one OpenAI-compatible API. Deepgram Nova-3 provides realtime STT; Workers AI backs it up, and Cloudflare runs the realtime Durable Object runtime.

Engineered for honesty

Schema-validated JSON, deterministic rule-engine fallback, TTS fallback chain, visible audit trail, and a documented “simulated vs live” boundary.

Privacy & safety

Consent-first, transient audio, scoped guardian access, no payments executed, fictional decoy persona.
